Tap a stock name to see all its figures. In tables, each cell shows the latest period on top and the one before it below; green and red show the change (for debt, a rise is red). Debt and reserves come from the balance sheet, which is yearly. Industry P/E is the median P/E of the peers Screener lists, and Sector P/E is the median of the companies on Screener's sector page. Pledged % is the share of promoter holding pledged (Screener shows nothing when it is zero). The score (0 to 100) ranks your stocks against each other on growth, margin, ROE, ROCE, P/E, debt, pledge and dividend yield. It is a sorting aid only, not advice. Updates only run while the app is open.
